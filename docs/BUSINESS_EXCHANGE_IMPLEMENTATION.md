# Business Exchange Implementation

Gobiz already had the core business listing and NDA access workflow. This iteration adds the missing investor side:

- Investor profile
- Investment budget range
- Preferred industry
- Preferred city
- Verification flag
- Investor-to-business matching service

Existing business exchange remains based on:
- listings.type = business/startup
- business_details
- business_access_requests

The next commercial layer is seller CRM and notification automation.
