# Gobiz 2.0 Execution Plan

## Vision
Transform Gobiz into an industrial marketplace combining products, RFQ, business exchange and investment opportunities.

## Phase 1 - Foundation
- Production security review
- RBAC and permissions
- Audit logs
- Company verification engine
- Database optimization

## Phase 2 - Marketplace
- Dynamic industrial specifications
- Advanced search
- RFQ matching engine
- Seller analytics dashboard

## Phase 3 - Revenue
- Subscription plans
- Featured listings
- Lead generation
- Seller CRM

## Phase 4 - Business Exchange
- Factory acquisition listings
- Company sale listings
- Startup marketplace
- NDA workflow
- Investor profiles

## Phase 5 - Intelligence
- AI assistant
- Product recommendations
- Buyer-seller matching
- Market analytics
